Glintwatch

Each player owns a lamp on one edge of a square reef; every turn each lamp fires a beam. Mirrors bend every beam by 90 degrees and belong to nobody.

  • Each turn, each player prints one action: PLACE x y type (type 0 = /, 1 = \) or WAIT.
  • Each turn, every crystal lit by a player's beam gives that player 1 point.
  • Only a lamp's owner may place mirrors in its yard (the cells near the lamp).
  • Each player has 12 mirrors. The game lasts 100 turns; the highest score wins.
  • Response time: 1000 ms on the first turn, 50 ms after.
