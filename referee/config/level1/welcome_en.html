Welcome to Glintwatch

Bend your lamp's beam with shared mirrors and hold the reef's crystals alone.

Night falls on the reef. Its crystals drink lamplight and keep it until dawn. You keep a lamp on the reef's edge and a bag of 12 mirrors.

How to play

  • Your lamp fires a beam in a straight line every turn. That line holds no crystal at the start.
  • Print one action per turn: PLACE x y type puts a mirror (0 = /, 1 = \), or WAIT.
  • A mirror turns every beam that reaches it by 90 degrees: yours and your opponents'.
  • Each turn, every crystal your beam lights gives you 1 point.
  • Only you may place mirrors in your yard, the cells near your lamp.
  • The game lasts 100 turns. The highest score wins.
  • Answer within 50 ms each turn (1000 ms on the first turn).

Tip: place one mirror on your beam's line so the beam turns towards a row or column of crystals.
