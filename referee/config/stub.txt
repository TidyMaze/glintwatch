STATEMENT
Light crystals with your lamp's beam: place mirrors to bend it.

INPUT
playerCount: 2 to 4
myId: your player id
dir: E, S, W or N, the direction your lamp fires
row: one row of the reef, y = 0 first
phase: tide phase of the crystal (-1: always awake)
mirrorType: 0 for /, 1 for \, -1 for an opponent's pending mirror
pending: 1 while the mirror does not bend beams yet
life: active turns left (-1: never wears)
crystalCount: number of crystals (one line each)
crystalLine: x y awake litBy then playerCount streaks, space-separated; split it (awake 1/0; litBy: player who lit it alone last turn, -1 nobody, -2 several; your streak is the (myId+1)-th streak)
turnCrystalCount: number of crystal lines this turn

OUTPUT
PLACE x y type | TURN x y | WAIT, optionally followed by a message
