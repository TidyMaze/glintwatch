Bend your lamp's beam with shared mirrors and hold the reef's crystals alone. 2 to 4 players.

This is a league based challenge.

For this challenge, multiple leagues for the same game are available. Once you have proven your worth against the Boss, you will access the higher league.

This league: The tide has come in. Each crystal sleeps 15 turns out of every 45 and forgets who held it. Crystals now hold 1 to 3 points of light, and a beam thrown into a rival's lamp pays 2 each turn.

 Goal

Score more points than the other lamp keepers by lighting crystals with your lamp's beam.

 Rules

The reef

The reef is a square grid of N x N cells (N is odd, 13 to 19). x is the column (0 on the left), y is the row (0 at the top); north is towards y = 0. 2 to 4 players play. Each player owns a lamp on one edge. The reef looks the same from every lamp (90-degree rotational symmetry). With 2 players the lamps are on opposite edges; with 3 players one edge has no lamp.

Cells:

  • . empty: a beam goes through;
  • 1 2 3 a crystal and its value: a beam goes through and lights the crystal;
  • A B C D the lamp of player 0, 1, 2, 3: a beam that reaches it stops;
  • # rock: a beam stops.

Beams

After all actions of a turn, every lamp fires one beam. It leaves the lamp in the lamp's direction and moves cell by cell until it leaves the reef, reaches a rock or reaches a lamp. Beams never block each other and a beam cannot loop. A beam that crosses the same crystal twice lights it once. Your lamp's straight line holds no crystal, rock or mirror at the start: to score, you must bend your beam.

Mirrors

A mirror turns a beam by 90 degrees:

MirrorBeam moving eastnorthwestsouth
type 0 = /leaves northleaves eastleaves southleaves west
type 1 = \leaves southleaves westleaves northleaves east

Mirrors belong to nobody: a mirror bends every beam that reaches it, yours and your opponents'. Players cannot remove mirrors. Mirrors wear out (see below).

Actions

Each turn you print one action:

  • PLACE x y type: put one of your mirrors on an empty cell.
  • TURN x y: flip a mirror (/ becomes \ and back).
  • WAIT: do nothing.

You may add a message after your action, for example PLACE 5 3 1 hello (30 characters max).

Yards. Each lamp's yard is the 5 x 3 block of cells around it (the lamp cell, and every cell up to 2 cells away along the edge and up to 2 cells deep, diagonals included). Only the yard's owner may place or turn mirrors there. Beams cross yards freely.

Same cell. If two or more players place a mirror on the same cell in the same turn, all of them fail and keep their mirror. If several players turn the same mirror in the same turn, it flips once.

Locks. Each mirror has a lock. You can turn a mirror only when its lock is 0. The lock is set to 5 when the mirror becomes active or is turned, then drops by 1 at the end of each following turn (so it reads 5 on the next turn's input).

Impossible actions are replaced by WAIT (the reason is shown in the game summary): a cell outside the reef, a type other than 0 or 1, a cell that is not empty (rock, crystal, lamp, mirror, pending mirror), a cell in another player's yard, no mirror in hand, turning where there is no active mirror or the lock is not 0. A line that is not a valid command (keyword and whole numbers) eliminates you.

Scoring

Each player has a streak (0 to 4) on each crystal. Each turn, for each crystal:

  • if the crystal is asleep: all streaks on it become 0 and nobody gains;
  • lit by exactly one player: that player's streak grows by 1 (max 4) and the player gains value x streak; every other player's streak drops by 1 (min 0);
  • lit by two or more players: nobody gains; those players keep their streak; the others' drop by 1;
  • lit by nobody: every streak drops by 1 (min 0).

A crystal you keep alone pays x1, x2, x3, then x4 every turn.

Mirror economy

  • Supply. You start with 4 mirrors and get 1 more at the start of turns 4, 8, 12 ... (the input of that turn already counts it). You hold at most 6; extra mirrors are lost.
  • Build time. A mirror placed on turn t is pending on turn t+1 and bends beams from turn t+2. While pending, beams go through its cell, nobody can place there or turn it, and your opponents read its type as -1 (you read the real type). A pending mirror shows lock 0 and life 20.
  • Wear. An active mirror bends beams for 20 turns, then shatters at the end of its 20th turn; its cell becomes empty. life gives the turns left.

Tides, values and strikes

  • Tides. Each crystal is awake 30 turns out of every 45: on turn t it is awake when (t + phase) % 45 < 30, otherwise asleep. phase is given at the start. An asleep crystal gives nothing and resets the streaks on it.
  • Values. Crystals are worth 1, 2 or 3.
  • Strike. Each turn, a beam that ends on another player's lamp gives 2 points to the player who fired it.

Order of a turn

  • Supply (before the input is sent).
  • All placements, then all turns.
  • Every lamp fires.
  • Scoring.
  • Locks drop by 1.
  • Pending mirrors become active, active mirrors wear.

End of the game

The game ends after 150 turns, or when fewer than two players remain. Equal scores share the rank. An eliminated player gets a score of -1; its lamp becomes a rock and its yard stays protected.

Victory Conditions
  • Have the highest score when the game ends.
Defeat Conditions
  • Have a lower score than another player when the game ends.
  • Your program does not answer in time, crashes, or prints a line that is not a valid command (keyword and whole numbers): you are eliminated.

Example (an example reef, not a real map)

N = 13. Your lamp is at (0,3) and fires east; a crystal of value 1 is at (5,6). You print PLACE 5 3 1. Your beam goes (1,3) ... (5,3), turns south on \, crosses (5,4), (5,5), lights (5,6) and goes on south. If nobody else lights it you gain 1, 2, 3, then 4 per turn. The mirror starts bending beams 2 turns after you place it.

 Game Input/Output

Initialization Input

Line 1: two space-separated integers playerCount and myId: the number of players (2 to 4) and your player id (0 to playerCount - 1).

Line 2: an integer N: the width and height of the reef.

Next playerCount lines: three space-separated integers and a character, playerId lampX lampY dir: a player's id, the cell of its lamp, and the direction its lamp fires (E, S, W or N).

Next N lines: a string row of N characters, one row of the reef from y = 0 down: . empty, # rock, 1 2 3 crystal of that value, A B C D lamp of player 0 1 2 3.

Next line: an integer crystalCount: the number of crystals.

Next crystalCount lines: four space-separated integers x y value phase: a crystal's cell, its value and its tide phase

Input for one game turn

Line 1: an integer turn: the turn number, starting at 1.

Next playerCount lines, by player id: three space-separated integers score mirrorsInHand active: the player's score, the mirrors it can still place, and 1 if it is in the game (0 if eliminated).

Next line: an integer mirrorCount: the number of mirrors on the reef.

Next mirrorCount lines: six space-separated integers x y type lock pending life. Every listed mirror, pending or active, fills its cell: you cannot place there.

  • x y: the mirror's cell.
  • type: 0 (/) or 1 (\); -1 for an opponent's pending mirror.
  • lock: turns before it can be turned; 0 when it can be turned
  • pending: 1 if not yet bending beams, else 0
  • life: active turns left before it shatters

Next line: an integer crystalCount: the number of crystals.

Next crystalCount lines: 4 + playerCount space-separated integers x y awake litBy s_0 ... s_(playerCount-1):

  • x y: the crystal's cell.
  • awake: 1 or 0
  • litBy: the player who lit it alone on the last turn; -1 nobody (and on turn 1); -2 several players.
  • s_i: player i's streak on it, 0 to 4
Output for one game turn

A single line with one action, optionally followed by a space and a message (30 characters max):

  • PLACE x y type: place a mirror of type type (0 or 1) on cell (x, y).
  • TURN x y: flip the mirror on cell (x, y).
  • WAIT: do nothing.

Tokens are separated by spaces; any other separator (a tab, for example) makes the line invalid.

Example: PLACE 5 3 1 hello

Constraints
2 ≤ playerCount ≤ 4
13 ≤ N ≤ 19, N is odd
Response time for the first turn ≤ 1000 ms
Response time for one turn ≤ 50 ms
