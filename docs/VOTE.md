Three strongest arguments for Glintwatch:
- vote-community: "Glintwatch is more original (no CG game has beams or mirrors) and has a one-line pitch and glowing beams that are fun to watch. It also has a ready design draft."
- cg-contrib-coder: "Deterministic O(N^2) beam trace per turn, fast referee, exhaustive tests easy, draft ready."
- vote-analyst: "Glintwatch is deterministic, symmetric and measurable: rank-over-seeds and greedy vs search gaps can be tested before shipping."

Three strongest arguments against Bulwark Accord (runner-up):
- cg-contrib-coder: "Diplomacy adjudication is a known bug farm (circular moves, support cut by the target, retreat clashes, 4-way bounces) = highest risk of a referee bug in the arena."
- vote-analyst: "Kingmaking and implicit alliances in 4p, with no negotiation channel, make ranking noisy."
- vote-level: "A hex map has no clean 90-degree 4-seat symmetry, so a fair 4-seat region graph is hard to build."

Dissent: vote-hardness (round 9) voted against Glintwatch: "Glintwatch is perfect-information with an easy greedy floor. Bulwark's risks can be fixed in design, but Glintwatch's low hardness can't."
