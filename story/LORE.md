# Glintwatch: lore bible (v1, 2026-11-11)

Canon for every player-facing text. The I/O and the rules keep plain words (crystal, mirror, yard, strike,
streak, tide); in-world names below are for flavour only, never in input/output or rule text.

## The world
The Tidereef is a shallow reef that only shows itself at night. Its crystals, called **glints**, drink lamplight
and keep it until dawn, when the shore towns buy the stored light. Four keeper houses hold lamps on the four
edges of the reef. Every night they bend their light through mirrors to fill as many glints as they can.
This night duty is the Glintwatch.

## The four houses (seat = player id in 4-player games)
| Seat | House | Colour | Emblem | Voice (for flavour lines) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | House Sky Ring | Sky `#56B4E9` | ring | calm, patient, speaks of circles and returns |
| 1 | House Amber Spire | Amber `#E69F00` | triangle | proud, direct, aims high |
| 2 | House Jade Keep | Jade `#009E73` | square | careful, builds walls of habit |
| 3 | House Orchid Star | Orchid `#CC79A7` | 4-point star | quick, bold, loves the far shot |
Short forms: Sky, Amber, Jade, Orchid. The houses are equal: no house is the hero or the villain.

## Every rule has a reason
| Rule | In-world reason |
|---|---|
| Lamps fire straight from the edge; the lamp's line starts empty | The Old Accord cleared each lamp's line so no house is born rich. |
| Mirrors bend every beam and belong to nobody | Once a mirror is set in the reef, the reef owns it. |
| Rocks stop beams | Black reef rock drinks light. |
| Yards (2 cells around a lamp) | The footing around a lamp is the house's own ground; only its keepers walk there. |
| A crystal pays only when one beam lights it | A glint takes one colour. Two lights fight inside it, it clouds and cracks, and stores nothing. |
| Streak x1 to x4 | A glint held by the same light warms up and stores more each turn, up to full warmth. |
| Losing a crystal drops the streak by 1 | An unlit glint cools slowly; a contested one freezes as it is. |
| Values 1-3 (Silver+) | Bigger glints hold more light. The heart of the reef holds the biggest one. |
| Mirror lock 5 turns after place or turn | A moved mirror sinks into soft sand and must settle before it can be turned again. |
| Two places on one cell fail | Two divers reach the same spot, collide, and both swim back with their mirror. |
| Supply +1 every 4 turns, max 6 (Bronze+) | Divers bring up one shell-mirror at a time; a keeper can carry 6. |
| Build delay 2 turns, type hidden (Bronze+) | A new mirror is set under a cloth while the coral grips it; rivals cannot see its angle. |
| Wear: 20 turns (Bronze+) | Salt eats the silver; after 20 turns the mirror shatters. |
| Tides: awake 30 of 45 turns (Silver+) | The tide rises over each glint in its own rhythm; under water it sleeps and loses all warmth. |
| Strike +2 (Silver+) | Light thrown into a rival lamp dazzles its keeper; the Accord pays the shot. |
| Elimination: lamp becomes rock, yard stays | A lamp gone dark is a dead stone, but its ground stays sacred. |
| Empty seat in 2-3 players | That edge has no house tonight; its ground is open to all. |
| Game end at turn 100/150 | Dawn. The towns count the stored light. |

## In-world names (flavour only)
glint = crystal, Glintwatch = the game and the duty, Tidereef = the board, shell-mirror = mirror,
yard = yard (same word), dazzle = strike, warmth = streak, tide = tide, dawn = game end.

## Tone
Quiet night, cool colours, light as the only warm thing. Short sentences. No violence beyond "dazzle".
No real places, brands or existing game lore.
