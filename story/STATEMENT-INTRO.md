# Statement story openers (one per league; designer owns the statement and decides)

Each opener is 2-4 lines, sits above "### Goal", and names only the rules that league unlocks.

## Wood 2
Night falls on the Tidereef. Its crystals drink lamplight and keep it until dawn.
You keep a lamp on the reef's edge and a bag of 12 mirrors. Bend your beam onto the crystals:
each turn, every crystal your light touches pays you.

## Wood 1
The reef is not empty any more: black rocks drink light, and a crystal lit by two lamps clouds over.
A crystal held by your beam alone warms up and pays more each turn, up to x4.
Mirrors can now be turned, but a moved mirror needs 5 turns to settle.

## Bronze
The divers bring up only one mirror every 4 turns, and you can carry 6.
A new mirror takes 2 turns to set, hidden from your rivals, and the salt breaks it after 20 turns.
Every mirror is a choice now.

## Silver
The tide has come in. Each crystal sleeps 15 turns out of every 45 and forgets who held it.
Crystals now hold 1 to 3 points of light, and a beam thrown into a rival's lamp pays 2 each turn.
